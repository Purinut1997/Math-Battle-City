# Art Prompts for Math Battle City: Operation Number Shield

**Visual Style Guide:** Modern 16-bit HD Pixel Art, Nature overgrown sci-fi ruins, vibrant green and gray tones.

Use these prompts in Midjourney (v6) or DALL-E 3 to generate the sprite assets for the game.

### 1. Player Tank
> **Prompt:** A top-down view of a futuristic sci-fi tank in modern 16-bit HD pixel art style. The tank is painted in vibrant green and gray tones, with subtle vines and moss growing on its armor, suggesting nature reclaiming technology. The tank has a distinct main cannon and glowing neon green energy vents. Flat 2D top-down perspective, isolated on a pure white background for sprite sheet extraction.

### 2. Enemy Tanks (Normal & Enraged)
> **Prompt:** A top-down view of two sci-fi enemy tanks side-by-side in modern 16-bit HD pixel art style. Both are covered in overgrown nature and vines. The left tank is painted in rusty gray and muted green with a menacing, mechanical look. The right tank is an "enraged" variant, glowing with intense bright red neon energy and emitting small red sparks. Flat 2D top-down perspective, isolated on a pure white background.

### 3. The Main Base
> **Prompt:** A top-down view of a high-tech sci-fi command base in modern 16-bit HD pixel art style. The base is an advanced metallic bunker partially reclaimed by nature, with thick green moss, glowing cyan energy panels, and a large central holographic projector displaying numbers. Flat 2D top-down perspective, isolated on a pure white background.

### 4. Boss Tank (Level 10)
> **Prompt:** A massive, intimidating boss tank from a top-down perspective in modern 16-bit HD pixel art style. The boss tank is heavily armored, colored in dark iron and vibrant green, completely overgrown with wild sci-fi vegetation. It features multiple heavy cannons, glowing red energy cores, and a digital holographic display on its chassis. Flat 2D top-down perspective, sprite sheet style on a pure white background.

### 5. Environment Tiles (Walls, Water, Bush)
> **Prompt:** A 2D top-down tileset for a video game in modern 16-bit HD pixel art style. The tileset includes: 1) A cracked brick wall covered in moss, 2) A reinforced futuristic steel wall with glowing neon lines, 3) A dense, vibrant green sci-fi bush/forest canopy, 4) A square tile of glowing, animated blue-green water. Arranged neatly on a grid, pure white background.

### 6. Power-Ups
> **Prompt:** A collection of 4 small video game power-up icons in modern 16-bit HD pixel art style. 1) A glowing bomb with a Pi (π) symbol, 2) A glowing futuristic shield shaped like a protractor, 3) A high-tech digital calculator emitting freeze rays, 4) A glowing green health cross with a plus (+) symbol. All hovering and glowing, isolated on a pure white background.
